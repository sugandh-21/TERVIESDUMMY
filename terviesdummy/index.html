Our Story

Celebrating the Art of Indian Craftsmanship

TerviesDummy was born out of a deep love for India's rich textile heritage. We work directly with master weavers and artisans from Varanasi, Jaipur, Surat, and Kanchipuram to bring you authentic, handcrafted ethnic wear.

Every piece in our collection tells a story — of skilled hands, age-old techniques, and a commitment to keeping India's weaving traditions alive for the next generation.
